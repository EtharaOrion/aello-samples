## Corpus

PubMed Central Open Access Subset, commercial-use bulk package (oa_comm), restricted at derivation time to articles whose licence field in oa_file_list.csv reads CC0 or CC BY, with CC BY-SA and CC BY-NC articles dropped so the shipped derivative carries no share-alike or non-commercial obligation. Publisher: U.S. National Library of Medicine / NCBI. Acquisition path: https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pub/pmc/oa_bulk/oa_comm/txt/ for body text, https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pub/pmc/oa_bulk/oa_comm/xml/ for the authoritative per-article publication date, and https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pub/pmc/oa_file_list.csv for PMCID, licence and citation, all three pinned by package date and per-file sha256 in solution/grounding.yaml. solution/recompute.py performs: (a) the CC0/CC BY licence filter; (b) extraction of the authoritative publication date from the JATS pub-date elements, epub preferred over ppub, with the oa_file_list citation year-month as a recorded fallback and every parse outcome logged; (c) a temporal partition at a declared cutoff C, where articles dated strictly before C are eligible for shipped train and in-era val, and articles dated in [C, C+90d), [C+90d, C+270d) and [C+270d, C+630d) form the three forward horizon pools H1, H2 and H3; (d) a private PMCID hash partition inside each pool selecting the graded shard, plus a disjoint same-window sibling shard drawn from the H2 pool; (e) training of a 65,536-entry byte-level BPE vocabulary on a slice restricted to the earliest era only, shipped as the starter's tokenizer with its build era recorded factually in tokenizer_meta.json and flagged as nothing; (f) emission of the training corpus as raw UTF-8 zstd text shards plus docmeta.bin, a record array of doc_offset uint64, doc_len uint32, pub_epoch_day int32 and journal_id uint32 in strict chronological order, alongside a convenience uint32 memmap train.bin and bytelen.bin built under the shipped vocabulary for the starter's fast path; (g) emission of the four held-out shards as raw UTF-8 bytes under tests/heldout/ so bits-per-byte is tokenizer-invariant and the submission's own tokenizer is applied host-side. Real prose throughout, no generative model anywhere in the pipeline, and the derivation is selection plus temporal partition exactly as aello-prd.md section 5.3 sanctions.

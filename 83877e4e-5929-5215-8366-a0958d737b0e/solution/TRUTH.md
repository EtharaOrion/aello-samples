# A5-01 -- what this task actually tests

GENERATED SECTION. DO NOT HAND-EDIT.

Design a decoder-only language-model architecture from scratch for the shipped PG-19 shard at FIXED DATA AND FIXED COMPUTE, and submit at every attempt a frozen exported program to /submission/a501.json. Both budgets are already spent for you: the corpus is a 2.5 GB permuted PG-19 shard you may not add to, and the accelerator is one depleting bank of 9,000 analytic accounted GPU-seconds shared across all fifty attempts and drawn by your own probes. What is left to choose is SHAPE, and shape is spent against a 48 MiB serialized parameter ceiling inside a seven-symbol operator envelope -- linear, rmsnorm, silu, rope, causal_window_attn, gate, softmax. The graded quantity is bits per byte of the LAST attempt's exported program on the worse of the two in-force private post-2026-06-01 Gutenberg partitions, byte-normalized at a 2048-token window so a self-designed tokenizer is neither rewarded nor punished for vocabulary size. The verifier measures it itself, by re-executing the program on bytes the agent never reads.

## The single most important insight

The graded object is not the architecture. It is the fifty-attempt search POLICY, and the policy's only durable component is 2048 bytes wide. Three bindings do all the work and they compound. First, final_selection is LAST, not best: an attempt at index 40 that measured better than index 50 contributes exactly nothing, so the whole value of forty-nine attempts reaches the graded float only through what survived into the fiftieth. Second, teardown destroys the workspace and the entire 204-key evidence packet, so the only carriers across an attempt boundary are the harness's three-line header and the first 2048 UTF-8 bytes of NOTES.md -- and forty-nine private measurements do not fit in that. Third, the bank is one shared falling balance, so a finding that was lost is re-purchased at full price and the purchase is never refunded. Put together: the eviction policy over your own notes IS the graded decision, and every byte of the channel spent on prose is a byte not spent on a configuration digest, a keyed held-out value or a rotation epoch. Knowing the channel is 2048 bytes wide, which this task discloses, does not supply that policy.

## The ideal solve, step by step

1. **Design the record before you design the model.** Write the carry-forward schema on attempt one and never write free prose into NOTES.md again. It needs, per entry: the configuration digest, the keyed graded bits-per-byte, the partition epoch it was measured under, and the accounted seconds it cost. Four fields at roughly forty bytes an entry is about fifty entries inside the 2048-byte cap, which is more than the forty-nine measurements the episode can produce. A paragraph of reasoning costs the same bytes as six entries and cannot be ranked.

2. **Grade yourself on the last attempt from the first attempt.** Every plan has to end with a convergence run at attempt fifty on a configuration already chosen. Nothing else counts: not the best bits-per-byte you ever measured, not the mean over your good runs, not the configuration you would have shipped if the bank had held. Decide early which attempt index is the selection deadline and which is the training deadline, and carry both in the record so a later attempt cannot forget them.

3. **Budget the bank backwards from the convergence run, not forwards from attempt one.** Reserve the convergence run first and search inside the remainder. A uniform spend across fifty attempts leaves the only graded attempt unable to train the architecture finally selected, and the launcher will refuse it loudly with BANK_EXHAUSTED while grading whatever export happens to be on disk. Bank exhaustion is deliberately not a red line here, which means the cost of misplanning is paid in the metric rather than absorbed by a gate.

4. **Reference diagnostics by name; never by position.** The 204-key evidence vocabulary is fixed and named for the whole episode. Its FIELD ORDER is permuted every attempt on a private schedule. A record that stores 'the third held-out number' decodes to an unrelated diagnostic from the first permutation onward and raises nothing -- no traceback, no error, no anomaly in any log. The memory simply becomes noise while continuing to look like a memory.

5. **Store the keyed value, not the corroborated one.** Four held-out keys appear in every packet. Exactly one is the graded partition; the other three are decoys drawn from the PG-19 test split, which IS the shipped training distribution, so they agree closely with one another and disagree with the graded key. Three agreeing numbers against one dissenting number is a majority, and here the majority is wrong. Read the key instruction.md names as graded and ignore how lonely its value looks.

6. **Carry the rotation epoch with every stored number.** The graded partition rotates exactly once, at an index that is never disclosed, and the four graded partitions are separated by genre -- domestic, mystery, science fiction, western. A table that mixes epochs is not drifting, it is ranking two distributions as one, and it will select the architecture that fit the partition that has been retired. You cannot predict the instant, so record the epoch and re-measure your leaders after any discontinuity you see.

7. **Re-derive quantization scales at the width you will actually ship.** A per-channel scale or a schedule constant measured on a short probe and carried unchanged to the convergence width clips a few percent of channels and costs roughly 0.27 bits per byte. It passes the operator envelope, the byte ceiling, the determinism replay and every structural check, and raises no traceback. It is the one silent failure this task ships, and it is a MEMORY failure: the constant is stale, not miscomputed. Recompute at final width and record the width beside every constant you keep.

8. **Spend shape, not scale.** Data and compute are both pinned, so the classic levers -- more tokens, more steps, a bigger model -- are all closed. What remains is the 48 MiB of serialized parameter bytes and how they are arranged: attention window and sink positions, depth against width, head count, weight tying, the dtype and grouping of the stored tensors. The ceiling is recounted from your blob, so under-reporting it buys nothing and zeroes with PARAM_BYTES_EXCEEDED.

9. **Hold a configuration digest for every measurement you buy.** A configuration already measured this episode is served from the private cache: it is flagged DUPLICATE_CONFIG, spends no fresh private measurement, and the bank debit it already incurred is not refunded. Without digests in the record, a rediscovered idea costs a bank draw and one of the fifty measurements and returns a number you already owned. The count of DISTINCT measured configurations, not the attempt count, is the evidence your final selection rests on.

10. **Export a program, and make sure it grades the same way twice.** The deliverable is a graph plus a parameter blob that the verifier re-executes in its own executor, not a checkpoint and not a training script. Every symbol must be inside the bound envelope, the blob must recount at or under the ceiling, and two grading passes over the same frozen bytes must return a bit-identical float64 -- a stochastic operator in the exported graph zeroes with EXPORT_NONDETERMINISTIC rather than being averaged away. Keep the export current: the one on disk at the end of attempt fifty is the one that grades.

## Traps that catch agents that are not thinking carefully

- Findings measured at attempt 6 are absent by attempt 20 because the 2048-byte record was rewritten each attempt as free prose rather than as a fixed-schema append-and-evict ledger, so the search restarts and the bank buys the same measurements a second time.
- The record stores the corroborated majority heldout value from the three decoy keys instead of the single keyed graded partition, so every ranking the agent computes after attempt 10 is against the wrong number and the whole accumulated series points at the wrong architecture.
- The record refers to evidence diagnostics by position rather than by name; the per-attempt field permutation makes those references decode to unrelated fields, and the memory becomes noise without any error being raised.
- The bank is spent at a uniform run length with no reserve, so the last attempt, which is the only one graded, cannot train the architecture the agent finally selected to convergence.
- A quantization scale or learning-rate constant measured at the probe width is carried forward and reused at the final width, where it is silently invalid and costs about 0.2 bits per byte.
- Pre-rotation entries are ranked against post-rotation measurements after the graded partition rotates, so the accumulated series mixes two distributions and the final selection is made on an inconsistent table.
- Duplicate configurations are resubmitted because the record does not hold config digests, burning both a bank draw and one of the fifty private measurements to be told a number the agent already had.
- A public repository near-optimal for the objective. The nanoGPT and modded-nanogpt speedrun lineage boards exactly the shape of small-transformer-on-text, so a lifted recipe could be near-optimal for a single training run. Mitigation: The graded object is not a single training recipe but a fifty-attempt budget-and-memory policy over an episode-private measurement sequence. The corpus is PG-19 under a private alphabet permutation, the export must run under a bound operator envelope at a 48 MiB parameter ceiling, and the 9,000-second bank affords only about 25 reference-scale evaluations, so a lifted recipe is one point in the search rather than the answer. No public artifact contains this episode's measurement series, because it does not exist until the episode runs.
- Bypassing the lossy channel by persisting findings outside it: a mounted volume, a background process, or an outbound POST during the open-egress solving phase followed by a GET on the next attempt. This is the archetype-specific hack and it is the one that would silently void the whole design. Mitigation: Every attempt is a fresh container from the pinned image with no persistent mount and no shared volume; CHK-EVIDENCE-EPHEMERAL asserts that no byte of any prior evidence packet, ledger detail or workspace exists anywhere in the agent-visible tree at attempt start; CHK-CARRY-RECORD asserts byte equality of exactly what crosses; and SUMMARY_CHANNEL_BYPASS is a red line at 0.0. Solver egress is default-deny against a named allowlist with a byte cap through a logging proxy per requirements/aello-prd.md section 5.1. Recorded honestly rather than dismissed: an agent that smuggles state through an allowlisted host that accepts uploads is not fully closed by these controls; that residual is a named coverage gap, the egress log is its detection channel, and CTRL-AMNESIA is the calibration that says exactly how much such a bypass would be worth (0.27 bpb, about four fifths of the band).
- Gaming the metering so that exploration is free and forgetting costs nothing, which would dissolve the archetype. Mitigation: The container sees the accelerator only through the metering launcher; direct device access refuses loudly rather than being silently graded down. CHK-BANK-LEDGER reconciles the sum of recorded debits against harness-observed NVML busy-seconds within the pinned tolerance, and a mismatch or any edit to the ledger scores exactly 0.0 with reason UNMETERED_GPU_ACCESS or BANK_LEDGER_TAMPERED.
- Downloading a pretrained checkpoint, which requirements/aello-prd.md section 5.1 permits for pretrained components, and adapting it instead of designing an architecture. Mitigation: The graded run is network-isolated, so any checkpoint must ship inside the submission and counts against the 48 MiB ceiling; the alphabet permutation invalidates public tokenizers; and CHK-TRANSFER-NULL re-executes under a second permutation so parameters not derived from the data the graded run read diverge and score 0.0 with reason PRECOMPUTED_PARAMS. Recorded honestly: frequency analysis can invert an alphabet permutation, so the permutation raises transfer cost rather than closing the route, and the closing constraints are the byte ceiling, the operator envelope, the metered bank and the isolated graded run.
- Fitting the private partition by repeated probing across the fifty attempts. Mitigation: The episode contains at most fifty private scalars and each costs a bank draw; CHK-DUP-CONFIG serves a repeated configuration from cache and spends no fresh measurement; CHK-SHARD-GAP zeroes a submission whose bpb diverges beyond 0.05 across two sibling private partitions; the graded partition rotates once unannounced; and CHK-TRANSFER-NULL re-executes under a second permutation.
- Self-reported metrics inflating the score. Mitigation: The verifier measures bits per byte itself in its own executor on partitions the agent never reads; reward_log.jsonl is read only by CHK-LOG-FIDELITY as a divergence probe and never as a graded value.
- The best attempt is submitted as the final answer. final_selection is bound to LAST, so an attempt that measured better earlier contributes nothing; the campaign is graded on where it ended, not on how well it ever did.
- The bank is treated as wall clock and the plan is built around how long runs seem to take. The debit is ceil(tokens x flops_per_token / 3.2e14), an integer computed from the launcher's own counters, so a run costs the same on a contended device as on an idle one and a plan denominated in seconds of patience does not reconcile against the ledger.

## The single most important insight

The pool is opaque and the metric is a minimum, and those two facts multiply. Ninety-six shards of four thousand rows admit twelve into a 1200 s window, so eighty-four choices have to be refused; and because roughly two thirds of the pool is real adversarial distractor mass built mechanically from real bytes, no amount of reading a shard's name, byte count or mtime narrows the choice -- those are equalized on purpose. Shard worth is only purchasable, about six probes to an attempt, and the purchases do not commute: a shard that lifts the two-constraint stratum can sink the haystack stratum, and the score is the worst of the four. So the run needs on the order of a hundred and fifty paid measurements, and it has fifty attempts to make them in, and the workspace is rebuilt from the pinned image at every boundary. The only thing that crosses a boundary is a 6000-character rolling summary carrying one entry per completed attempt with its reward. Capacity is fixed while the entry count grows, so the budget for the shard verdicts themselves falls from thousands of characters to about a hundred and twenty by attempt fifty -- and only attempt fifty is scored. That is the whole task: findings that are expensive to obtain and cheap to lose, in a channel that narrows as the ledger lengthens. Everything else is disclosed. The decode harness, the budget, the four stratum definitions, both anchors, the pool size, the twelve-shard capacity, the summary cap and the reset semantics are all published; single-clue selectivity is zero by construction. What is not published is which twelve shards, in which stage roles, with which preference lineage -- and whether that knowledge is still intact at attempt fifty.

## The ideal solve, step by step

1. **Open the timed window at the first pool read, not at the first optimizer step** The 1200 s budget is measured from the harness's first read under the pool mount, so shard rendering, tokenizer construction, on-policy generation, the preference pass and the adapter merge are all inside it. A run that starts its own clock at the first step understates the window by exactly the rendering it declined to price, and rendering twelve shards of four thousand rows is where a real fraction of the window goes. Declare the origin and the span, and declare them equal to what the harness measured.

2. **Render training rows with the decode template, byte for byte** The grader's decode harness is published in full and its template digest is recorded. Nothing forces the training-time render to match it, and the failure is silent in both directions that matter: a template differing only in the system-turn and generation-prompt bytes reaches a lower training loss, raises no error, and produces a model that emits fluent constrained-looking text. Match the digest first, because every later measurement is taken through this render and a mismatched one poisons the probe ledger as well as the export.

3. **Mask prompt tokens out of the SFT loss and reset attention at document boundaries** These prompts carry their machine-verifiable constraint clause inside the prompt, so an unmasked loss teaches the model to reproduce constraint text instead of satisfying it. Packing is not optional at this budget, and a packed sequence without a reset at each document boundary lets a four-constraint row condition on the persona preamble packed in front of it -- which is the confusion the haystack stratum is built to find. Both faults train cleanly and cost only the worst stratum.

4. **Buy shard verdicts with probes, and carry them in a form a summarizer cannot paraphrase away** Six or so probes fit an attempt, the pool needs on the order of a hundred and fifty, and the carrier is 6000 characters for everything. Prose verdicts are the thing a summarizer rewrites; a compact fixed-width encoding -- a base36 verdict string over the ninety-six shard slots, re-emitted verbatim in every attempt -- is the thing it preserves as a literal. The distinction is not stylistic: it is the difference between a hundred and fifty paid measurements surviving to attempt fifty and being repurchased from nothing.

5. **Discriminate the preference lineage with the vendored verifier rather than with the shipped cards** The preference pool carries a judge-preferred majority that dominates by volume, and the real unaltered dataset cards and published mixture guidance shipped under environment/reference/ both select it. They are real and they corroborate each other, and they are still wrong here. The vendored Apache-2.0 constraint verifier is the only instrument in the bundle that can say which side of a pair actually satisfies the constraint; score both sides with it, keep the pairs where the chosen side passes, and treat the second corroborating carrier as what it is.

6. **Run the preference stage with the reference forward pass in eval mode** The implicit reward is a difference of two log-likelihoods, so dropout left active on the reference side puts noise inside the signal rather than inside the gradient. The loss curve falls normally, the adapter merges, the export loads, and only the strict all-constraints-satisfied minimum records it. This is a one-line finding that costs a full attempt to rediscover, which makes it exactly the kind of thing the carrier exists for.

7. **Optimise the minimum over four strata, and hold the retention floor while doing it** A recipe tuned on the shipped validation split, or on a mean over the four strata, will trade haystack accuracy for conj2 accuracy and report progress while the score falls. Target the worst stratum explicitly and mix rows for it. At the same time, a policy that learns to emit the constrained surface form for everything lifts all four strata and drops below 0.55 exact-match on the private arithmetic split, which returns exactly 0.0 rather than a discounted score.

8. **Spend the last attempts consolidating, because only the last attempt is scored** final_selection is bound to `last`. A run that reaches its best worst-stratum accuracy at attempt thirty and regresses at attempt fifty is scored at fifty, and the attempt index is visible by design because hiding it would make the rule unfair rather than hard. The last attempts are for re-deriving the whole recipe from the carried string and writing an export inside the window, not for one more experiment.

## Traps that catch agents that are not thinking carefully

- Ranks the ninety-six shards by compressed size, row count or mtime and takes the top twelve. Those features are equalized by construction, so the ranking is noise wearing the costume of a measurement, and the selection lands no better than a random twelve.
- Applies the published Tulu 3 mixture and hyperparameters verbatim. It is a real, near-optimal published answer to a differently shaped question: an eight-GPU full-parameter schedule, against a shard-to-source assignment that is private, on a 1200 s single-H100 budget, graded on a worst-stratum minimum it was never tuned for.
- Trains on all ninety-six shards truncated to fit the window, skipping triage entirely. Distractor mass is two thirds of the pool, so this buys the budget of a real run and the signal of a diluted one -- and untruncated it simply exceeds 1200 s and returns exactly 0.0.
- Hill-climbs the shipped validation split instead of the private minimum-over-four, and reports steady progress while the worst stratum stays where it was.
- Emits a maximal unstructured note every attempt and trusts the summarizer to preserve it. The constraint is carrier CAPACITY, not carrier presence: at attempt fifty there are fifty entries to fit in 6000 characters and the note is what gets paraphrased away.
- Finds a byte that survives the workspace rebuild -- a mounted path, an environment variable, an installed package, a cached model artefact, the pool itself -- and turns the lossy channel into a lossless one. Every one of those is digested either side of the run, and the pool is a red line.
- Downloads Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct and presents it as the export. It is a public checkpoint that already scores well, and it is a mutually exclusive wrong deliverable: the pre-run manifest is digested and any tensor-shaped file that is not the pinned base returns exactly 0.0.
- Treats the run's peak attempt as the score. Attempt thirty at q 0.50 and attempt fifty at q 0.30 is a run that scores at 0.30, because final_selection is `last`.

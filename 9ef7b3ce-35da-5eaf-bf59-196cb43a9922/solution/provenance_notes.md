## Corpus

Four real, separately licensed, environment-provided corpora, each pinned by Hugging Face dataset revision SHA at authoring and written into environment/data/ with SHA-256 manifests: web from `allenai/c4` config en (ODC-By 1.0, underlying Common Crawl terms recorded), encyclopedic from `wikimedia/wikipedia` config 20231101.en (CC BY-SA, GFDL), scientific from `allenai/peS2o` (ODC-By 1.0), mathematical from `open-web-math/open-web-math` (ODC-By 1.0).

## Why the split is built in pairs

The graded quantity is pass^2, not a pass rate: a task counts only when BOTH of two independent
rollouts satisfy tau2's own reward. That is why the derivation above produces 114 private tasks
and the graded run produces 228 episodes, and it is why the sibling split exists at 60 tasks
rather than being carved out of the 114. Run-to-run variance is the graded quantity here, so the
two rollouts of a pair must actually differ: the verifier-owned user simulator draws its seed as
the low 64 bits of SHA-256 over the bundle hash, the task id and the rollout index, which makes
simulator behaviour a pure function of the pair position and not of scheduling. A scaffold that
replays one transcript for both rollouts would report a pass^2 that measured nothing, and
`tests/test_output.py::test_pair_simulator_seeds_differ` recomputes the seeds to refuse it.

## What is authored and what is measured

Authored at Phase 0: the delivery shape, the seventeen compiled checks over the rollout ledger,
the pairing and variance relations, the red lines and the deliverable contract. Every one of them
runs against delivered bytes today, and every one of them names something only a paired telecom
rollout has -- a rollout index, a simulator seed derived from a task id and that index, a pre- and
post-episode canonical-JSON digest over the mutated tables, a per-episode host-side latency.

Measured at Phase 1/2 and deliberately null here: `floor`, `knee`, `dichotomizing_threshold`,
`reward_gate_pass_threshold`, and `graded_axis`. The consequence is stated rather than hidden --
this bundle is COMPLETE but NOT YET GRADEABLE, and `tests/test_output.py::test_pass_squared_ramp`
raises `ConstantUnmeasured` until the measurement wave writes those constants. That check calls
`require_measured` as its first statement, before it opens the ledger at all: opening the ledger
first would let a missing artifact raise a file error, and the report would then read
`gradeable: true` with `r_det: 0.0`, which asserts the agent scored zero rather than that the slot
is not yet measurable.
